# Group A: US small/mid-size family Labrador breeder website audit

Method note: pages were fetched via WebFetch (text-to-markdown plus summarizer), so visual layout, mobile behavior, and photo style were NOT directly observed; only structure, text, and nav. Audit date context: 2026-11-21. Only the pages named were opened (usually one page per site, not the full site). Sites that failed: spragueriverlabradors.netlify.app/litter and /contact (HTTP 404; only seen in search snippet, which said deposits by cash/Venmo/Zelle and phone-only contact, unverified). Westlane (Jotform questionnaire only; no main site found). Rocky Ridge, Hedge Lane, Skyline Hill, Jackson Woods, Labs To Love, Da-Koda High were seen only as GoodDog directory snippets, not audited.

## Which sections do nearly all good breeder sites have vs. which are rare but impressive?

### Takeaway

Across 7 sites, the near-universal elements were a litters page, dog/parent pages, a contact page, a health guarantee, and a deposit rule. Rare and impressive: per-litter health tables with certificate numbers and DNA panel, per-litter status labels, a written reservation process, and a visible update date. Many sites were thin or stale.

### Cited Findings

- Heatherdowns Labradors (mid-size "ranch", English Labs, many litters; not strictly a hobby kennel). Nav: The Ranch, The Girls, Studs & Stud Services, Upcoming Litters, New Puppy Family, Adult Labs To Offer, Training programs, Testimonials, Health Guarantee, Contact, webstore with cart icon. One long Upcoming Litters page, 15 litters, each with parent health panel (PRA-PRCD, CNM, EIC, cystinuria, copper toxicosis, dilute clear, OFA hips/elbows/cardiac/patella). Page also showed a "Updated as of 11/21/2026" line. Fetched page was truncated, so later sections (testimonials, contact) not seen. — [Heatherdowns](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Sunflower Hill AKC Labs (small home breeder, Google Sites). Nav: Home; Labrador Puppies (Available Puppies, Future Upcoming Litters, Reserving a Puppy); About Us; Testimonials; Puppy Care (care, Health Guarantee, NuVet); Photo Gallery/Videos; one page per dam (Daisy, Everest); Stud Dog page; Contact. Weekly puppy photos on Facebook/Instagram/YouTube. — [Sunflower Hill, Reserving a Puppy](https://sites.google.com/view/sunflower-hill-akc-labs/labrador-puppies/reserving-a-puppy)
- Wildrose Kennels (large multi-location kennel in MS/TX/NC/WI, British Labs; included as a contrast, not a small breeder). Nav: About, Puppy Deposit, Journal, Experience, Our Labs (Sires, Dams, Mating Calendar, Puppies, Started/Finished), Training Videos, Store, Location; Instagram/Facebook/email footer; phone numbers per location. — [Wildrose](https://koala-fife-5gaw.squarespace.com/puppies)
- Brown Dog Acres (tiny hobby hunting-line chocolate breeder, NW Iowa, Google Sites). Home page lists sire and dams with hunt titles (HRCH, MH) and a pedigree link per dog; litters handled by linking to Hunting Lab Pedigree. — [Brown Dog Acres](https://sites.google.com/view/browndogacres/home)
- Labradors of Griffin Keep (home breeder, Google Sites). Nav: Home, About, LGK Labs, Current/Upcoming Litters, Gallery (sub-page per dog), Contact. — [Griffin Keep](https://sites.google.com/view/labradorsofgriffinkeep/currentupcoming-litters)
- Ridgetop Retrievers (hunting/field-trial breeder, Google Sites). Nav: Home, Contact, Litters, Our Dogs, Photos. — [Ridgetop](https://sites.google.com/view/ridgetopretrievers/litters)
- Arctic Texan Labradors (small natural-rearing breeder, Palmer, Alaska, Google Sites). Largest content set: Upcoming litters, Happy Families, article library (feeding, coat colors, newborn care, natural rearing), Our Boys/Our Girls, Puppies (Buying a Puppy, Older pups, Questionnaire, Terms of Purchase, "Identifying a Reputable Breeder"), Retired Forever Loved page with 15 retired dogs. — [Arctic Texan](https://sites.google.com/site/arctictexanlabradors/home)
- Health detail varied widely. Best: Ridgetop lists OFA hips/elbows with certificate numbers, CERF/CAER eyes, EIC/CNM clear by test or parentage for both parents. Heatherdowns shows full DNA panel and OFA per litter (some marked "Prelims"). Sunflower Hill, Wildrose, Griffin Keep, Brown Dog Acres pages showed no specific results (some link to separate health pages not fetched). Arctic Texan says "health-tested" and gives guarantee terms but no results on the page fetched. — [Ridgetop](https://sites.google.com/view/ridgetopretrievers/litters); [Heatherdowns](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies); [Arctic Texan](https://sites.google.com/site/arctictexanlabradors/home)
- Guarantees: Ridgetop 26-month health guarantee; Griffin Keep 2-year genetic guarantee plus 1-week guarantee, lifetime take-back policy, requires buyer to purchase a bag of the breeder's food; Arctic Texan 2-year hips/elbows, lifetime genetic guarantee, prepaid microchip and AKC registration, 30 days free insurance, a Terms of Purchase page. — [Ridgetop](https://sites.google.com/view/ridgetopretrievers/litters); [Griffin Keep](https://sites.google.com/view/labradorsofgriffinkeep/currentupcoming-litters); [Arctic Texan](https://sites.google.com/site/arctictexanlabradors/home)
- Testimonials: dedicated pages exist at Sunflower Hill, Heatherdowns, Wildrose ("Wildrose Pack"), Arctic Texan ("Happy Families"); none on the pages for Ridgetop, Griffin Keep, Brown Dog Acres. Contents of the testimonial pages were not fetched.
- Contact: Brown Dog Acres phone + personal AOL email + Instagram; Ridgetop phone, email, Facebook/Instagram; Griffin Keep email only, no phone, no social; Sunflower Hill Gmail + Contact page; Arctic Texan Gmail, two phones, Facebook, Instagram; Wildrose phone per location plus email. (URLs as above.)

### Inferences

- Near-universal: litters/upcoming page, parent/dog pages, health guarantee, deposit policy, contact. Rare but strong: certificate-number health tables, DNA panel per litter, status labels, a stated reservation workflow with contract, retired-dog memorial page, buyer-education articles, per-dog pedigree links, an explicit "last updated" date.
- Free Google Sites and Squarespace templates dominate small breeders; generic Gmail/AOL addresses are common.

### Gaps

- Photo galleries, mobile rendering, and typography could not be judged from text-only fetches.
- Testimonial and health-guarantee page contents not opened for most sites.
- Only 6 small/mid sites plus 1 large contrast site audited; the 404 Sprague River site and Westlane could not be audited.

## How do they show prices and deposits? How do they handle waitlists?

### Takeaway

Prices are mostly hidden (contact for price); deposits are almost always stated and non-refundable, $400 to $500 for small breeders. Waitlists are handled by email, phone, or a questionnaire form; formal waitlists with forms are the minority.

### Cited Findings

- Heatherdowns shows $3,450 per puppy on most litters (one litter "email/text for pricing"), mentions discounts for partial payments, gives no deposit amount, and no on-page reserve instructions; reservations are shown as lists of named buyers and open pick positions ("2nd and 3rd Picks AVAILABLE"). — [Heatherdowns](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Sunflower Hill: $400 non-refundable deposit by Zelle (or a GoodDog payment link), then a Google Form questionnaire, then a signed Puppy Deposit Agreement (.docx) emailed back; breeder promises reserved puppy not sold to others; price not listed. — [Sunflower Hill](https://sites.google.com/view/sunflower-hill-akc-labs/labrador-puppies/reserving-a-puppy)
- Griffin Keep: $500 non-refundable deposit credited to price, transferable to another litter, puppies assigned in order of deposit filtered by sex/color; no price; email to request a spot; may ask for vet references. — [Griffin Keep](https://sites.google.com/view/labradorsofgriffinkeep/currentupcoming-litters)
- Ridgetop: non-refundable deposit reserves "pick", phone to reserve, no price, no form. — [Ridgetop](https://sites.google.com/view/ridgetopretrievers/litters)
- Wildrose: deposit amount not on page; deposit stays with the dam and can transfer to another litter; reserve by deposit page or phone. — [Wildrose](https://koala-fife-5gaw.squarespace.com/puppies)
- Arctic Texan: no prices; waitlist joined by copying the Puppy Questionnaire into an email; explicitly stopped accepting GoodDog applications. — [Arctic Texan](https://sites.google.com/site/arctictexanlabradors/home)
- Brown Dog Acres: no prices, no deposit text, call to reserve. — [Brown Dog Acres](https://sites.google.com/view/browndogacres/home)
- Westlane (only questionnaire pages seen, per search result): $500 non-refundable deposit holds puppy until pickup at about 9-10 weeks, Limited Registration on all pups, Jotform questionnaire "last updated 06/09/2026". — [Westlane questionnaire](https://form.jotform.com/westlanedogs/westlanes-questionnaire) (seen only via search snippet, not opened)

### Inferences

- Pattern: deposit amount public, total price private. Heatherdowns is the exception with public price.
- Forms are commonly hosted outside the site (Google Forms, Jotform), so the site itself has no application workflow.

### Gaps

- No site was seen with an on-site waitlist position/counter; Heatherdowns' named-reservation lists are the closest.

## How do they keep litter info current and signal "this page is up to date"?

### Takeaway

Most do it poorly. Stale pages were the dominant flaw; only Heatherdowns and Arctic Texan showed update dates.

### Cited Findings

- Heatherdowns: "Updated as of 11/21/2026" banner and status labels ("Reservations Full!!!", "Reservations Available", "Breeding Pending", "Breeding Completed", whelp/release dates). But internal errors: some release dates read "January 2026", one whelp date "June 2027 2026", a 12/04/2025 date, copy-pasted sire text. The banner date equals today's date, so it may be hand-edited or auto-generated; unverified. — [Heatherdowns](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Arctic Texan: "Last updated 11 Jan 2026"; availability notes dated ("as of 11 Jan 2026 one adult needs placement"); but litter history items are 2014 and 2017 and "Upcoming litters" has no dates. — [Arctic Texan](https://sites.google.com/site/arctictexanlabradors/home)
- Griffin Keep: page titled "Current/Upcoming Litters" holds only a litter born March 29, 2023 (go-home May 24, 2023); "Page updated" label with no date. — [Griffin Keep](https://sites.google.com/view/labradorsofgriffinkeep/currentupcoming-litters)
- Ridgetop: "Current Litter" born July 24, 2019, marked "SOLD OUT" yet still says "Call now to reserve your pick". — [Ridgetop](https://sites.google.com/view/ridgetopretrievers/litters)
- Brown Dog Acres: "Page updated" blank; "expecting litters" with no dates. — [Brown Dog Acres](https://sites.google.com/view/browndogacres/home)
- Wildrose externalizes to a Google Doc "Mating Calendar" with no visible date. — [Wildrose](https://koala-fife-5gaw.squarespace.com/puppies)
- Several sites offload litter status to Hunting Lab Pedigree or Facebook/Instagram (Sunflower Hill weekly photos, Ridgetop and Brown Dog Acres pedigree links). Hunting Lab Pedigree has a listing format with puppy pages: [example](https://huntinglabpedigree.com/puppy.asp?id=68491) (appeared in search only, not opened).

### Inferences

- Google Sites shows a platform-generated "Page updated" label that is blank/invisible, so it gives no trust signal; a visible, dated "As of" line plus status chips is the best-observed approach, but needs error-free dates.
- Stale "current litter" pages from 2019 and 2023 were found live in 2026, a major trust anti-pattern.

### Gaps

- Could not verify whether Heatherdowns' date is auto-generated.

## What design/photo conventions make a site feel trustworthy and warm?

### Takeaway

Limited evidence because only text was captured. Text-level signals of trust: named dogs with titles and pedigrees, certificate numbers, retired-dog tributes, explicit policies, real contact details, and consistent dates. Distrust signals: stale dates, copy-paste errors, placeholder slugs, personal/free emails.

### Cited Findings

- Arctic Texan has a "Retired, Forever Loved" section (15 dogs), "Happy Families" page, buyer-education articles, and a "Identifying a Reputable Breeder" page, and displays AKC Bred with H.E.A.R.T., Puppy Culture, and GoodDog "Excellent" health rating badges. Photo copyright notice present. — [Arctic Texan](https://sites.google.com/site/arctictexanlabradors/home)
- Brown Dog Acres foregrounds hunt titles and links a pedigree for every dog. — [Brown Dog Acres](https://sites.google.com/view/browndogacres/home)
- Heatherdowns uses family photos with captions, a "New Puppy Family" page, and a "Pup De'PAW'sits" webstore; flaws: nav slugs like "health-guarantee-1" for Testimonials and "new-page-1", typos in health terms, repeated menus, "Heatherdowns Reserved" placeholder images, one very long page. — [Heatherdowns](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Sunflower Hill: inconsistent business name, inconsistent pickup wording ("8 weeks" vs "8th week"), no update date, two payment methods unexplained. — [Sunflower Hill](https://sites.google.com/view/sunflower-hill-akc-labs/labrador-puppies/reserving-a-puppy)
- Wildrose: footer links point to a squarespace staging domain and multiple sister domains, a malformed "Https://www" link, a misspelled slug, 2019-era images. — [Wildrose](https://koala-fife-5gaw.squarespace.com/puppies)
- Brown Dog Acres and Ridgetop footers show Google Sites "Report abuse" links; Ridgetop Facebook links carry tracking redirect parameters. (URLs above)

### Inferences

- Platform choice (free Google Sites, Squarespace trial subdomains) is visible to buyers via URLs like sites.google.com/view/... and *.squarespace.com, which weakens perceived professionalism; no custom domain was observed for the audited pages (the Squarespace URLs may be staging aliases of custom domains; unverified).

### Gaps

- Photo style, color/typography, mobile layout, and Flash-era design not assessable; none of the sites fetched appeared Flash-era from text, but that cannot be confirmed.

## Synthesis: best-practice examples worth copying and anti-patterns

### Takeaway

Copy: Heatherdowns per-litter status chips with whelp/release dates and per-litter health panels; Ridgetop's OFA certificate-number listing; Sunflower Hill's 3-step reservation (deposit, questionnaire, signed agreement); Arctic Texan's terms page, retired-dogs page, buyer-education content, and dated availability notes. Avoid stale "current litter" pages, contradictory status text, and unexplained prices/deposits.

### Cited Findings

- Best-practice: status vocabulary (Breeding Pending / Reservations Available / Reservations Full / Breeding Completed / whelped with count) — [Heatherdowns](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Best-practice: explicit reservation steps and written agreement — [Sunflower Hill](https://sites.google.com/view/sunflower-hill-akc-labs/labrador-puppies/reserving-a-puppy)
- Best-practice: deposit transfer-between-litters rule, order-of-deposit assignment — [Griffin Keep](https://sites.google.com/view/labradorsofgriffinkeep/currentupcoming-litters); [Wildrose](https://koala-fife-5gaw.squarespace.com/puppies)
- Best-practice: certificate-level health data on parent profiles — [Ridgetop](https://sites.google.com/view/ridgetopretrievers/litters)
- Anti-patterns: 2019/2023 litters labeled "current"; "SOLD OUT" plus "call to reserve"; blank "page updated"; no health data; no price or deposit; "stay tuned" with no dates; external Google Doc calendar; placeholder slugs; email-only contact with no phone (Griffin Keep); personal AOL email (Brown Dog Acres). (URLs as above)

### Inferences

- A site template for Snow Goose Kennels should include: litter cards with status badge, whelp/go-home dates and an "as of" date; parent profiles with health clearance rows and registry links; reservation page with deposit amount, what it covers, refund/transfer rule, and an application form; policies/contract page; testimonials; gallery; phone + email + social.

### Gaps

- Rest of group A's candidate list (Rocky Ridge, Hedge Lane, Skyline Hill, Jackson Woods, Labs To Love, Da-Koda High, Drunken Bear Ranch, Crown Royal Hunting Labs) were found only as GoodDog directory entries and were not opened on their own sites: [GoodDog directory example](https://www.gooddog.com/breeders/rocky-ridge-labradors-minnesota).
