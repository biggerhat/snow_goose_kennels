# Group B Lab breeder site audit (established / professionally built / outside US)

NOTE ON COVERAGE: Target was 7-8 sites incl. 2+ outside US. Only 5 kennel sites were actually opened and read (details below); 1 is a thin brand landing page. Search tooling surfaced mostly directory pages (Champdogs, Good Dog, Paction) rather than kennel sites, and Champdogs returned HTTP 403. No UK kennel's own site was opened, so UK design contrast is NOT covered. Two Canadian sites were opened. Several "findings" below come from an automated page-summarizer (WebFetch), so treat exact details (dates, link slugs) as reported-by-tool, not eyeballed. No visual/mobile rendering was verified for any site.

## Which sites did I open, and what do they show about breeder size/type and reputation?

### Takeaway

Only Heatherdowns is clearly a large, long-running, business-scale kennel. The rest are small-to-mid kennels on template builders (Squarespace, Google Sites, WordPress-like, Showit). I found no genuine field-trial / dual-champion kennel site I could open.

### Cited Findings

- Heatherdowns Labradors, Ltd. (Franktown, Colorado): corporate-style kennel with phone, street address, partner vet clinics, stud services, adult dogs, training programs; markets "Champion/Grand Champion pedigrees" (unsupported on the page) — [Upcoming Litters](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies), [Health Guarantee](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/health-guarantee)
- Twisted Clovers Labradors (Edgewood, NE Iowa): small program, "English Lab"/AKC; sires sometimes off-site — [Your Questions](https://twisted-clovers-labradors.squarespace.com/your-questions)
- Apple Valley Labradors (Canada, CKC member, Google Sites): small; Dog Delivery Service page — [Purchase Info](https://sites.google.com/applevalleylabradors.ca/applevalleylabrador/home/puppies/purchase-info), [Health Guarantee](https://sites.google.com/applevalleylabradors.ca/applevalleylabrador/home/health-guarantee)
- Lucky Labs (Ontario, Canada; domain chocolatelabradorretriever.ca): self-described "small scale family based registered breeder", claims 17+ yrs, CKC; delivers to Toronto/Windsor — [Lab puppies for sale](https://chocolatelabradorretriever.ca/lab-puppies-for-sale)
- Ridgemere Retrievers (Ohio): new (about 7 months on Good Dog at time of profile), breeder is a vet tech; Good Dog certified — [Good Dog profile](https://www.gooddog.com/breeders/ridgemere-retrievers-ohio); site: [Showit site](https://ridgemere-retrievers-1.showit.site/)

### Inferences

- "Premium" look (Showit, Squarespace) does not equal premium information; Ridgemere's designed site was nearly empty of buyer-critical facts.

### Gaps

- No field-trial/dual-champion kennel site opened; no UK kennel site opened (Champdogs 403; sandylands.co.uk guess returned nothing usable; Gunnerspeg kennel site not found).

## How do they surface health testing?

### Takeaway

Best practice seen is per-dog lists of OFA hips/elbows/eyes/heart plus a DNA panel, but even the big kennel pushes certificates to separate Girls/Studs pages and uses inconsistent abbreviations. One Canadian kennel listed 19 DNA conditions but no hip/elbow info; another showed none.

### Cited Findings

- Twisted Clovers lists OFA hips, elbows, eyes, hearts plus EIC, PRA, CNM, HNPK, Cystinuria as text in the FAQ; vet exam at 7 weeks — [Your Questions](https://twisted-clovers-labradors.squarespace.com/your-questions)
- Heatherdowns shows genetic panels inline in each litter block but sends readers to Girls/Stud pages for certificates; abbreviations inconsistent (HNPK/HPNK, EHLERS1 vs EHLERS2), some OFA marked "Prelims" without final link — [Upcoming Litters](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Apple Valley: breeding dogs tested for 19 listed genetic conditions (DM, Narcolepsy, Stargardt, PRA...) per the Health Guarantee page; no hip/elbow info seen on the two pages read; also claims Bio Sensor early stimulation — [Health Guarantee](https://sites.google.com/applevalleylabradors.ca/applevalleylabrador/home/health-guarantee)
- Lucky Labs: no parent health testing stated on the page read (only puppy vet check, microchip, 4-year written guarantee) — [page](https://chocolatelabradorretriever.ca/lab-puppies-for-sale)
- Ridgemere home page: no health testing mentioned despite "thoughtfully bred" — [site](https://ridgemere-retrievers-1.showit.site/); the Good Dog profile (third-party) lists OFA good hips, normal elbows, heart/eye clear, PRA, EIC, HNPK, CNM for the dam — [Good Dog](https://www.gooddog.com/breeders/ridgemere-retrievers-ohio)
- Directory snippets (not site visits): UK breeders cite BVA/KC hip and elbow scores (e.g., 3:4 / 0:0), EIC and CNM tests — [Champdogs search results](https://www.champdogs.co.uk/breeds/labrador-retriever/breeders?ht=2)

### Inferences

- No site opened used badges or direct OFA database links; per-dog text lists were the norm.

### Gaps

- Could not verify OFA/CHIC links, badges, or UK BVA display on a kennel's own site.

## How do they present application/waitlist processes, deposits, and timelines?

### Takeaway

Process pages are thin. Most sites only have a "Wait List"/"Apply" link or button with no steps. Deposits are usually either missing or only partly stated.

### Cited Findings

- Apple Valley: $50 waitlist deposit + $500 down payment, balance due at 7 weeks, 2026 price $2,800 incl. HST, e-transfer/cash; application process not described on the page — [Purchase Info](https://sites.google.com/applevalleylabradors.ca/applevalleylabrador/home/puppies/purchase-info)
- Heatherdowns: prices $3,450 on most litters, one litter "email or text"; deposit amount never stated; no visible application form; publishes buyer names with reservation dates per litter (privacy concern) — [Upcoming Litters](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Lucky Labs: process is "apply, join waiting list, get emailed photos"; first-come; deposit amount and refund terms not stated; every "APPLY NOW" goes to a contact anchor; no price on page (sent to another page) — [page](https://chocolatelabradorretriever.ca/lab-puppies-for-sale)
- Twisted Clovers: nav has Available > Prices, Puppies, Wait List, Dog Application; steps not described on FAQ page — [Your Questions](https://twisted-clovers-labradors.squarespace.com/your-questions)
- Ridgemere: only an "inquire now" button; Good Dog profile states $1,000 non-refundable deposit, $3,000-3,400 balance, 2-yr guarantee, Klarna financing — [Good Dog](https://www.gooddog.com/breeders/ridgemere-retrievers-ohio)

### Inferences

- Apple Valley's explicit deposit/balance/timing is the clearest money statement of the set.

### Gaps

- No "how it works" page opened; Heatherdowns/Twisted Clovers application forms not opened.

## What written policies do they publish, and how prominently?

### Takeaway

Heatherdowns publishes the most detailed policy (own nav item) but it is restrictive; Twisted Clovers has buyer-friendly lifetime return in FAQ. Pick order was not addressed anywhere I read.

### Cited Findings

- Heatherdowns guarantee: genetic/hereditary only; remedy mainly replacement pup with first pick; 1-year window for fatal congenital, 30 months for hip dysplasia; spay/neuter not before 18 months; 3-day return for refund excluding deposit; voided unless buyer buys NuVet 12 months, orders TLC food, activates Trupanion, vet visit by 2nd vaccine, never places dog in a shelter — [Health Guarantee](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/health-guarantee)
- Twisted Clovers: spay/neuter recommended at 18-24 months; any dog may be returned at any point in its life, no questions asked; limited AKC registration on pet pups, breeding rights after discussion; visits to meet parents allowed; puppies stay until 8+ weeks — [Your Questions](https://twisted-clovers-labradors.squarespace.com/your-questions)
- Apple Valley: non-breeding contract mentioned, terms not shown; spay/neuter, return, pick order not on pages read — [Purchase Info](https://sites.google.com/applevalleylabradors.ca/applevalleylabrador/home/puppies/purchase-info)
- Lucky Labs: written 4-year health guarantee and 6 weeks insurance (Canada), terms not summarized; lifelong support promised — [page](https://chocolatelabradorretriever.ca/lab-puppies-for-sale)

### Inferences

- Tying guarantees to product purchases (supplements, food) is a visible trust-eroding pattern.

### Gaps

- Pick order policy and co-ownership policy not found on any opened site.

## How is litter availability presented, and what photo/video/storytelling conventions appear?

### Takeaway

Heatherdowns uses per-litter blocks with status lines (Breeding Pending, Reservations Full, AVAILABLE) and named reservations by sex; execution was error-prone. Storytelling is emotive and thin on facts.

### Cited Findings

- Heatherdowns litter blocks: sire x dam, status headline, reservations listed by sex with dates, price, health block; but status headlines contradict body text, impossible whelp/release dates (e.g., whelp Nov 2026, release Jan 2026), stale 2025 reservations, copy-paste errors, shouting caps/emoji, buyer family photos, reused/mismatched captions — [Upcoming Litters](https://heatherdowns-labradors.squarespace.com/our-puppies)
- Lucky Labs: litters with "expecting mid November" next to already-born ones; yellow-available vs "no yellow litters" contradiction; "website is up to date" yet "availability changes daily"; 4 "forever home" entries link to the same YouTube video; testimonials/gallery live on Facebook — [page](https://chocolatelabradorretriever.ca/lab-puppies-for-sale)
- Ridgemere: brand storytelling ("raised with intention", "Meet the queen"), video/image blocks, no litters/prices on home; nav top vs footer inconsistent, "#/" placeholder links, "@2030 copyrighted" — [site](https://ridgemere-retrievers-1.showit.site/)

### Inferences

- Dated or contradictory availability is the single biggest untrustworthy signal; a single dated "last updated" and archiving past litters would avoid it.

### Gaps

- Mobile experience and actual visual design not assessed (text-only extraction). Testimonial pages not read.

## Synthesis (for the report writer)

### Takeaway

Within this limited sample, the "premium" differentiators are: standalone detailed guarantee page in main nav, per-litter health blocks, phone/address/vet partners (Heatherdowns), explicit deposit/balance timing (Apple Valley), plain-language lifetime-return and spay/neuter guidance (Twisted Clovers). Anti-patterns are plentiful.

### Cited Findings

- (see above; all sources listed per section)

### Inferences

- Worth copying for a small kennel with no contracts: one page for price + deposit + balance timing; per-dog health list with registry links; a FAQ stating spay/neuter advice and return policy; a visible dated "last updated"; real contact block; litter cards with a single clear status (Planned / Born / Reserved / Sold-out).
- Anti-patterns: repeated menu copies, link slugs not matching labels, impossible/stale dates, public buyer names, unexplained "Apply" buttons going to contact anchors, missing contact/price, guarantee voided by required product purchases, unsupported "champion lines" claims, placeholder/template leftovers (cookie banner tokens, "#/" links, wrong copyright year), color-genetics errors.

### Gaps

- Needs follow-up: 2+ UK kennel sites, a real field-trial/dual-champion kennel site, mobile review, and professional-site comparison.
