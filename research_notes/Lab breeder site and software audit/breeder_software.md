# Dog breeder management software: feature and pricing comparison (researched 2026-11-21)

Method note: vendor pages were fetched where possible. Capterra, G2 and Software Advice pages returned HTTP 403, so aggregator data comes from search-result snippets only. No Reddit threads or full review text were retrieved. Prices are "as seen on the date/source given" and several conflict.

## Which features are baseline vs differentiators, and what does each product offer (comparison table)

### Takeaway

Baseline across nearly all dog products: heat/breeding tracking, litter and puppy records, weight logs, health/vaccine records, pedigree, contacts. Differentiators: public waitlist/application forms, buyer portal, e-signed contracts, online payments, hosted kennel website, multi-user access. Only vendor-confirmed facts are tabulated; blanks mean unverified, not absent.

### Cited Findings

Feature prevalence from the vendor-verified products (BreedTools, BreedTracker, BreederBuddy, Breedera, PedFast, BreederHQ-secondhand):

- Standard (nearly all): heat/breeding tracking, litter and puppy records, weight logs (all but BreederBuddy page lists explicitly), health/vaccine records, contact management.
- Common: pedigree tools (all but Breedera page), waitlist (BreedTools, BreedTracker, BreederBuddy, Breedera, BreederHQ, Kinvara), contract tools (BreedTools, BreedTracker, BreederBuddy, Breeders Companion, Kinvara, Breeder Cloud Pro), buyer portal (BreedTracker, BreederBuddy, BreederHQ, Kinvara), growth charts (BreedTools, BreedTracker, Breedera), expense/income (BreedTools P&L, BreedTracker invoices, PedFast, Breeders Companion, BreederHQ), data export (BreedTools, BreedTracker, Breedera, PedFast partly), multi-user (BreedTools up to 5, BreederBuddy, Breedera custom tier).
- Rare: online deposits/payments (BreederBuddy, BreederHQ marketplace; BreedTracker invoices only), hosted kennel website (BreedTools Pro, BreederBuddy Pro), progesterone tracking (BreedTools Pro, BreedTracker), microchip/registration tracking (only BreedTracker's registration-paperwork import seen; otherwise not verified), automated emails/texts (BreederBuddy emails; push alerts BreedTools; others unverified).

### Inferences

- Baseline is a reproduction/health record keeper; money (deposits, portal, e-sign) is what separates $9-15 tools from $30-79 tools.
- Newer entrants (BreedTools, BreedTracker) put waitlists/applications/portal on free tiers, undercutting older $29-39 tools.
- Vet reminders and automated texts/emails were not documented clearly by any vendor, so treat as unknown/rare.

### Gaps

- Per-feature matrices for Kinvara, KennelPlus, Breeder Cloud Pro, Breeders Companion, BreederHQ, Empowered Breeder not verified (vendor sites or Capterra blocked).
- Pricing for Breeders Companion and Breeder Systems not found; Kinvara/KennelPlus/BreederHQ prices come only from aggregators and conflict.
- Platform details for BreedTracker, Kinvara, Breeder Cloud Pro unverified; Puppy Spot/PuppyFind tools not researched.

## Which products target small hobby breeders and are mobile-friendly, and at what price

### Takeaway

Best hobby fits: BreedTools (free to $9/mo, PWA), BreedTracker (free to $14.95), Breedera (free to GBP 9.99, native apps only), Breeder Cloud Pro (from $6.99 per aggregators). BreederBuddy, Kinvara, BreederHQ cost $29-60+ and target more business-like operations.

### Cited Findings

- BreedTools PWA works on any device with offline use — [breedtools.com/app](https://breedtools.com/app)
- Breedera is iOS/Android only, no desktop — [breedera.com/pricing](https://breedera.com/pricing)
- BreederBuddy has native Android push notifications; iOS app pending, works in mobile browsers — [breederbuddy.com](https://www.breederbuddy.com)
- Breeder Cloud Pro reviewers are mostly small businesses and say it works on phones and laptops, including at dog shows — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/kennel/breeder-cloud-pro-profile/reviews/) (snippet)
- BreedTracker describes itself as for "serious and professional breeders" who outgrew spreadsheets, yet free tier covers 4 litters/yr — [breedtracker.com](https://www.breedtracker.com)
- PedFast is Windows desktop only — [pedfast.com](https://www.pedfast.com/breeders.html)

### Inferences

- Mobile-first hobbyist sweet spot is the free tiers of BreedTools, BreedTracker and Breedera.

### Gaps

- No verified App Store/Play data for BreederBuddy, BreederHQ, Kinvara mobile apps.

## What small breeders complain about

### Takeaway

Verified complaint evidence is thin. Themes seen: freezes/glitches, navigation and outdated tutorials, weak reporting, poor website integration, connectivity dependence, dated design, and cost; spreadsheets and Facebook groups remain the default.

### Cited Findings

- Breeder Cloud Pro reviewers report freezes, login trouble, editing glitches, hard initial navigation, outdated training material; ask for better reporting, easier puppy photo upload, PDF contract download, auto pedigree from scans; one says it integrates poorly with personal websites; rural connectivity concern — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/kennel/breeder-cloud-pro-profile/reviews/) (search summary, not full text)
- Praise: easy to learn, tutorial videos, records/pedigrees/contracts in one place, heat and vaccine tracking — same source
- Breeders track weights, vaccines, heats and waitlists in spreadsheets and Facebook groups; Breedera is "dated" — founder characterization of a competitor, not breeder quotes — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/breedtools/breeders-are-still-using-spreadsheets-in-2026-built-something-better)
- BreederHQ has 1 Capterra review; no reviews on AlternativeTo — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10049441/BreederHQ/) (snippet)
- Breedera Android ~2.9 vs iOS ~4.4 — see sources above

### Inferences

- Data lock-in is addressed by BreedTools (export on any plan, CSV), BreedTracker (export only on Pro), Breedera (export on free); PedFast lock-in is local database file.

### Gaps

- No Reddit r/breeding threads, G2 or App Store review text retrieved. Cost/data lock-in complaints are not evidenced by actual breeder quotes.

## Do any provide public-facing waitlist/application forms or website embeds

### Takeaway

Yes: BreedTracker (free tier: custom applications and public litter pages), BreedTools Pro (public litter pages, kennel website, application forms), BreederBuddy (applications; website on $60 Pro). No product verified offering an embeddable widget for an existing site.

### Cited Findings

- BreedTracker free: custom applications, public litter pages, private buyer portal — [breedtracker.com](https://www.breedtracker.com)
- BreedTools Pro: public litter pages, branded kennel website, application forms — [breedtools.com/app](https://breedtools.com/app)
- BreederBuddy: applications and waitlists in Standard; hosted website in Pro — [breederbuddy.com](https://www.breederbuddy.com)
- BreederHQ: onboarding builds your storefront; marketplace with deposits (secondhand) — [breederhq.com/pricing](https://www.breederhq.com/pricing)
- Breeder Cloud Pro reviewer says it integrates poorly with personal websites — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/kennel/breeder-cloud-pro-profile/reviews/)

### Inferences

- A breeder with an existing site (e.g. Snow Goose Kennels) would link out to hosted pages rather than embed; embed capability remains unverified.

### Gaps

- Embed/iframe/widget support not documented on any fetched page; Kinvara public-form capability unverified.
